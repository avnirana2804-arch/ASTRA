ASTRA Architecture Overview

Area Specific Thermal Resilience Analytics for HACR Environments

Core Objective

ASTRA models high-altitude troop shelters to optimize heating efficiency, integrate Phase Change Materials (PCM), and drastically reduce kerosene fossil fuel dependency in extreme sub-zero operational sectors like Siachen and Ladakh.

The 3-User Operational Models

1. Expert Mode (DRDO Scientists & Engineers)

  • Configurable Parameters: FEA mesh density, composite layer thermal conductivities, PCM enthalpy curve overrides, numerical boundary conditions.

2. Officer Mode (Field Commanders & Logistics)

  • Configurable Parameters: GIS map sector coordinates, automated NASA POWER weather API ingestion (solar irradiance, wind speed, ambient temp), troop capacity.

3. General Mode (Fast Presets)

  • Configurable Parameters: Preset shelter dimensions, standard troop occupancies (4/8/12-man tents), quick weather presets for rapid fuel consumption estimates.

Defense Research & Development Organisation

Ministry of Defence | Government of India

About DRDO

DRDO is the premier R&D wing of the Ministry of Defence, dedicated to empowering India with cutting-edge defense technologies and self-reliant indigenous military solutions.

High-Altitude Defense Research Focus
  • Extreme Environment Habitability: Designing passive and active thermal management systems for High Altitude Command Regions (HACR) like Siachen, Eastern Ladakh, and Tawang.
  • Energy Footprint Optimization: Replacing heavy kerosene/diesel logistical transport with smart thermal storage, phase change materials (PCM), and high-fidelity numerical simulation models.
  • Indigenous Software Solutions: Sponsoring platforms like ASTRA to give field commanders predictive climate modeling, thermal comfort analytics, and operational planning tools.
